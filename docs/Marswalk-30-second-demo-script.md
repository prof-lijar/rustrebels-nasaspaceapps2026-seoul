# SuperJar · Marswalk — 30-second demo video script
Rust Rebels · NASA Space Apps 2026 Seoul · Challenge: Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map

About 70 spoken words, a calm but brisk pace. Burn in English subtitles.
Record the game in 1920×1080 at 60 fps (OBS works). Turn sound on (N) so the real Perseverance wind and rover audio plays under the voice-over.

## Capture checklist
- Before you record: hit R (restart), then start auto-walk (P) so the camera moves smoothly with no keyboard jitter.
- Record every shot as its own 5–10 s clip and cut to the timings in an editor (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve or Premiere).
- Shots to grab: start screen · first-person pan · third-person walk with SuperJar · hazard tags appearing · expanded route map · storm banner · sheltering at the rover · flying SuperJar up to a wide aerial.
- Record the voice-over separately in a quiet room, then lay it over the game audio with the game at about −18 dB.
- Keep the full video at 30 s or less. Upload it to YouTube (unlisted is fine) and submit the public link.
